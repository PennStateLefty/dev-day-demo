# Synthetic personal auto policy

**Policy ID:** pol-demo-auto  
**Policy period:** 2024-03-15 through 2025-03-15

> Synthetic training material adapted from the Foundry Insurance Agents Workshop. It is not a real insurance contract or a coverage decision.

## Section 2.1 — Liability

Pays for bodily injury and property damage to others for which an insured is legally responsible arising from ownership or use of the insured vehicle.

## Section 2.2 — Collision

Pays for direct damage to the insured vehicle caused by impact with another vehicle or object, or by upset (rollover), regardless of fault. Subject to the synthetic demonstration deductible of USD 500.

## Section 2.3 — Comprehensive / other than collision

Pays for direct damage to the insured vehicle caused by perils other than collision, including theft, fire, vandalism, hail, falling objects, flood, and animal strikes. Subject to the synthetic demonstration deductible of USD 250.

## Section 2.4 — Glass

Windshield and window glass repair is described under comprehensive coverage. The source policy states that the deductible is waived for repair and applies to full glass replacement.

## Section 3 — Exclusions

This synthetic policy lists the following exclusions:

1. Damage while the vehicle is used for commercial ride-share or delivery without the relevant endorsement.
2. Loss due to racing or speed contests.
3. Intentional damage caused by an insured.
4. Damage from wear and tear, mechanical or electrical breakdown, or freezing.
5. Loss while the vehicle is driven by an excluded person or without a valid license.
6. Custom equipment beyond a USD 1,000 sublimit unless scheduled.

## Section 4 — Claims process

1. Report a claim within 30 days; theft or vandalism requires a police report and report number.
2. Provide the date, time, location, and description of the incident.
3. Provide photographs of damage and other-party details when applicable.
4. Obtain a repair estimate from an approved shop. The deductible is subtracted from any covered amount.

This policy text is provided only to test retrieval and citation handling. The demo must not determine coverage or make a claim decision.
